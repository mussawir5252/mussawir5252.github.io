Teaching

Film Writing

Upper-level. Cross-listed with Dramatic Arts and English.

A screenwriting course. Students develop original short screenplays from idea to polished draft and workshop each other’s pages. I teach it in fall 2025 and fall 2026.

Film Theory: Auteur Study

Upper-level.

A close-reading course built around individual directors and their bodies of work. Students analyze films in writing and in video essays. I taught it in spring 2025.
