Narrative short. Writer, director, editor, and producer. MFA thesis film.

Pay the Difference

On the eve of July Fourth, a broke handyman has one day to claim his son’s remains.

An intimate story about economic insecurity and bureaucracy. The film traces how administrative and financial pressure reshapes relationships, narrows choices, and alters a person’s sense of dignity. Rather than present bureaucracy as an abstraction or offer a social diagnosis, I use character-centered fiction to follow its intimate consequences and the human cost it exacts.

Narrative short, 2025. Eight minutes. Writer, director, and editor.

Worthy

A painting student resolves to either become a great painter in a single day or never paint again.

A character study told as a wordless montage, scored to Vivaldi’s “Winter,” holding to her perspective as she destroys her past work and pushes herself past her limits.

Narrative short, 2022. Eighteen minutes. Writer, director, editor, and producer. Urdu with English subtitles.

Color of Sunset

A photographer confronts the gradual loss of their vision, and what it means to see.

A photographer takes his student into the hills to teach him how to look. He has not told him it is the last sunset he will ever photograph. Long takes, an overcast sky, and one sunset. I made it as my thesis film at Beaconhouse National University with Chal Phir Cinema, a Lahore collective working to bring audiences back to independent film.
