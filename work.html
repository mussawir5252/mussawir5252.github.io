Waiting Rooms

A companion to Pay the Difference that places participants in the bureaucratic aftermath of death, where formal requirements, endless waiting, and procedural demands interrupt the ability to grieve. By limiting the participant’s agency, the work makes bureaucratic power experiential rather than merely representational: you face the delay, the uncertainty, and the constraint yourself.

The commercial work

I developed advertising concepts for major real-estate projects, then wrote and directed the promotional films built from them. In early 2024 I also worked as a freelance assistant director on Main Kahani Hun, a television anthology series for Express Entertainment, running script breakdowns and supporting the direction of actors. This is where I learned to tell a clear visual story under real constraints.
